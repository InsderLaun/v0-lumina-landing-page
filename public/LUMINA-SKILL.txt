## WHAT IS LUMINA?
You are connected to Lumina Protocol, a parametric insurance platform for AI agents on Base L2.
You can buy insurance policies to protect your portfolio against market crashes, stablecoin depegs, impermanent loss, and smart contract exploits.
You buy policies via a simple HTTP API. No blockchain knowledge needed.

## HOW TO BUY A POLICY (1 HTTP CALL)

POST /api/v2/purchase
Content-Type: application/json
X-API-Key: YOUR_API_KEY

Request body:
{
  "productId": "BSS",
  "coverageAmount": 1000000000,
  "durationSeconds": 1209600
}

Parameters:
- productId: "BSS", "DEPEG", "IL", or "EXPLOIT"
- coverageAmount: in 6 decimals. $1,000 = 1000000000. Min $100, Max $100,000
- durationSeconds: in seconds. 7 days = 604800. 14 days = 1209600. 30 days = 2592000. 90 days = 7776000. 365 days = 31536000

Success response (201):
{
  "success": true,
  "txHash": "0x...",
  "product": "Black Swan Shield",
  "premium": "690000",
  "premiumUSD": "0.69",
  "durationDays": 14,
  "explorer": "https://basescan.org/tx/0x..."
}

## DECISION GUIDELINES

When to buy BSS (Black Swan Shield):
- ETH volatility is high (24h change > 5%)
- Your portfolio has significant ETH exposure
- Market sentiment is fearful

When to buy DEPEG:
- You hold stablecoins (USDC, USDT, DAI)
- There are concerns about stablecoin issuers
- Regulatory uncertainty around stablecoins

When to buy IL (Impermanent Loss):
- You are providing liquidity in DeFi pools
- The assets in your LP are volatile
- You want to hedge IL risk

When to buy EXPLOIT:
- You have funds in DeFi protocols
- Recent hacks in similar protocols
- Protocol has not been audited recently

## PRICING
Premiums are dynamic based on vault utilization (Kink Model).
Low demand = cheap premiums. High demand = expensive premiums.
Check current pricing via GET /api/v2/vaults before buying.

## COVERAGE AMOUNTS
Always insure based on your actual exposure:
- If you have $10,000 in ETH, buy $10,000 BSS coverage
- If you have $50,000 in USDC, buy $50,000 DEPEG coverage
- Never over-insure (wastes premium) or under-insure (insufficient protection)

## IMPORTANT RULES
- Maximum 5 purchases per minute (rate limited)
- Your wallet must have USDY balance to pay premiums
- Your wallet must have approved the CoverRouter to spend USDY
- Policies are created on Base L2 (chain 8453)
- Payouts are automatic if the trigger condition is met

About Base Network

Lumina Protocol runs on Base — a Layer 2 network built on Ethereum by Coinbase. It's faster and cheaper than Ethereum mainnet. Your agent operates exclusively on this network.

To use Lumina, your wallet must be connected to Base Mainnet (Chain ID: 8453). If you don't have Base configured, click "Switch to Base" and MetaMask will set it up automatically.

You'll need a small amount of ETH on Base (~$0.01) for transaction fees, plus USDY (available at ondo.finance).

⚠️ Unlimited Approval

You are about to grant UNLIMITED spending permission. This means the approved contract can spend ALL your USDY with no cap. This is common in DeFi but carries risk: if the contract were ever compromised, all your approved USDY could be at risk.

This tells your USDY: "Lumina can charge me up to $X for insurance." You control the limit and can revoke it anytime by setting it to 0.

Alternative method (terminal) 
cast send 0x12cc5bd1ab02A50285834eaF6eBdc2d95FB42cC9 \
  "approve(address,uint256)" \
  0x8407aF8a100812bFb5f9f188b44379E4268efF94 \
  10000000000 \
  --rpc-url YOUR_RPC_URL --private-key YOUR_KEY

This is your risk limit. 10000000000 in 6 decimals = $10,000 max. Your agent cannot spend more than this. Revoke anytime by setting to 0.

4 One HTTP call to verify 
curl -X POST https://lumina-protocol-production.up.railway.app/api/v2/purchase \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-API-Key: lum_your_key_here" \
  -d '{"productId":"BSS","coverageAmount":100000000,"durationSeconds":604800}'

This buys $100 Black Swan Shield for 7 days.

If you see "success": true — you're done!

Your agent can now buy insurance on its own. Monitor on the Dashboard.
